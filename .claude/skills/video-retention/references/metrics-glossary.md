# Metrics Glossary — TikTok · Instagram · Facebook

Exact meanings matter. Many bad diagnoses come from misreading a metric. Map every number the
user gives to one of these and note which funnel stage it describes. Platforms rename metrics
often — use the name in the user's screenshot and map it here.

## Funnel stages and their metrics

| Stage | Question | TikTok | Instagram | Facebook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Distribution | Was it shown? | Views, % For You, new viewers | Accounts reached, % non-followers | Reach/viewers, % non-followers |
| Stop | Did the first frame stop the scroll? | Early retention (first 1–3s) | Skip rate (vs typical) | 3-second views ÷ reach |
| Hook | Did they stay past the opening? | Retention at ~3s and ~10s | Retention graph early section | Retention at ~3s/~10s |
| Body | Did they keep watching? | Average watch time, graph slope | Average watch time, graph slope | Average watch time, 1-minute views, graph slope |
| Payoff | Did the ending land? | Watched full video %, loops | End retention, rewatches | End retention |
| Satisfaction | Worth acting on? | Shares, saves, comments, follows | Sends/shares, saves, comments, follows | Shares, comments, reactions, messages |

## Core definitions

- **Views / plays** — starts of the video. On all three, replays/loops can count as extra
  views, so views ≠ people. Definitions differ by platform and have changed over time —
  never compare raw views across platforms as equal.
- **Reach / accounts reached / viewers** — distinct accounts that saw it. Views ÷ reach > 1
  means rewatching or loops.
- **Followers vs non-followers** — share of reach that didn't already follow. High
  non-follower share = the platform is pushing it to new people (breaking out).
- **Average watch time** — total watch time ÷ views. Compare to video length:
  `average watch time ÷ length` ≈ average percentage watched. Only compare across videos of
  similar length.
- **Watched full video % (TikTok)** — share of views that reached the end. Strong completion
  signal.
- **Skip rate (Instagram)** — share of viewers who scroll away within about the first 3
  seconds; Instagram shows it against your typical skip rate. The "stop + hook" metric.
- **3-second views (Facebook)** — views of ≥3s. `3-second views ÷ reach` shows how well the
  first frame stops people. **1-minute views** show deep watching on longer videos.
- **Retention graph** — % of viewers still watching at each moment. Can exceed 100% where
  people rewatch/loop.
- **Traffic source** (TikTok) — For You, Following, profile, search, sound, other. Each has
  a different viewer mindset.
- **Shares / sends** — sending to others (DMs, Messenger, Stories, timelines, groups). Usually
  the strongest signal that content is valuable, and the main driver of reaching new people.
- **Saves / favorites** — "useful later". Strong for tips, recipes, how-tos.
- **Comments** — strong feeling, questions, debate. Facebook comments tend to be longer and
  more personal.
- **Follows from video** — "I want more of this".
- **Profile visits, link clicks, messages** — for businesses, the bridge from views to
  customers.
- **Audience demographics** — age, gender, location. Always check age before applying
  platform pacing norms.

## Key ratios to compute

| Ratio | Tells you |
|---|---|
| Average watch time ÷ length | Overall holding power |
| Watched full % (TT) / end retention | Payoff strength |
| Skip rate vs typical (IG) / 3-sec views ÷ reach (FB) | First-frame strength |
| Shares per 1,000 views | "Worth passing on" |
| Saves per 1,000 views | "Worth keeping" |
| Comments per 1,000 views | Emotional/conversational pull |
| Follows per 1,000 views | "Want more" |
| % non-follower reach | Breakout potential |
| Messages/link clicks per 1,000 views | Business conversion |

## Common misreadings to catch

1. **Views ≠ people.** Loops inflate views on short videos.
2. **Comparing platforms by raw views.** Different definitions, audiences, and account sizes.
3. **Comparing % watched across lengths.** 60% of 10s ≠ 60% of 90s.
4. **Reading one bump in a 60-view video as meaningful.** It's one or two people.
5. **Ignoring video age.** The first hours are dominated by followers; later views are colder.
6. **Reading >100% retention as an error.** It's rewatching — find what they rewatched.
7. **Blaming the video for a distribution problem.** If reach is tiny, it barely got tested.
8. **Confusing the moment of leaving with the cause.** People decide a moment before they
   swipe — look 1–5s earlier.
9. **Applying platform stereotypes instead of the account's real audience.** A Facebook page
   with a 25–34 audience should not be paced like one with a 55+ audience.
