# Platform Notes

Platforms change features and metric definitions often. Treat these as general guidance and
defer to what the user's actual dashboard shows. If a metric name in the user's screenshot
differs from here, use theirs.

## YouTube long-form
- **Discovery**: Browse (home), Suggested, Search, Subscriptions, External. Recommendation
  weighs how viewers respond — whether they click, how long they watch, and signs of
  satisfaction (surveys, likes/dislikes, shares, returning).
- **Key analytics**: impressions, impressions CTR, views, AVD, APV, retention graph (absolute
  and relative), key moments (intro, spikes, dips), traffic sources, returning/new viewers,
  end screen CTR, subscribers gained.
- **Packaging tools**: title, thumbnail (with "Test & compare" A/B testing where available),
  chapters, description, pinned comment, end screens, cards.
- **Editing after publish**: YouTube Studio editor can trim and cut sections, blur, add
  audio (limitations apply). Cannot add new footage — that needs a re-upload.
- **Mindset**: viewers chose to click a specific video; the promise check is the first 5–15s.
- **Length**: no universal ideal. Longer videos accumulate more watch time if retention holds;
  padded videos lose. Match length to the content's genuine value.

## YouTube Shorts
- **Discovery**: Shorts feed mostly; viewers didn't choose it — it was served.
- **Key analytics**: views, engaged views (where shown), "viewed vs swiped away", AVD,
  retention graph (often exceeds 100% from loops), subscribers, likes, comments, shares.
- **Mindset**: thumb ready; the first 1–2 seconds decide everything.
- **Fixes**: first-frame action, instant hook text, cut everything before the hook,
  engineer loops, keep only the essential beats.

## TikTok
- **Discovery**: For You feed dominates; also Following, search, sounds, hashtags, profile.
- **Key analytics**: views, total play time, average watch time, watched full video %,
  new viewers %, traffic sources (For You, profile, following, search, sound), retention graph
  in analytics, likes, comments, shares, saves.
- **Mindset**: very fast judgment; sound is often on; native, less-produced styles can
  outperform polished ones; trends and sounds provide context.
- **Fixes**: hook in the first second, on-screen text, fast pacing, strong ending that invites
  rewatch or comment, relevant sound/trend when it fits.

## Instagram Reels
- **Discovery**: Reels tab, Explore, feed (followers), shares via DMs.
- **Key analytics**: plays/views, reach, average watch time, watch time, skip rate (where
  shown), likes, comments, shares, saves, follows. Sends/shares via DM are especially
  meaningful for reach.
- **Mindset**: similar to TikTok; aesthetic quality sometimes matters more; many viewers
  watch muted — captions and on-screen text are important.
- **Fixes**: strong first frame (it's also the grid cover), on-screen text hook, share-worthy
  or save-worthy content, clean loop.

## Facebook video / LinkedIn / X
- Often autoplay muted in feed → captions and visual hooks are critical.
- LinkedIn: professional framing, value in first lines of the post text, subtitles.
- X: short, punchy, news-like; first seconds must be self-explanatory.

## Cross-posting
- The same video can behave very differently per platform. Analyze each separately.
- Remove other platforms' watermarks when cross-posting (some platforms may de-prioritize them).
- Re-cut hooks per platform when possible: long-form hook ≠ Shorts hook.

## Business / local-service videos (bakeries, salons, trades, etc.)
- Goal is usually bookings/sales, not raw views. Track clicks to site, DMs, bookings, and
  profile visits alongside retention.
- Low views from a local, relevant audience can beat high views from irrelevant ones.
- Strong formats: transformation (before → after), process ("satisfying" steps), behind the
  scenes, customer reactions, "a day making X orders", price reveals, mistakes & fixes.
- Put the CTA at the peak moment ("DM 'CAKE' to book yours for the weekend") and in the
  caption/pinned comment.
