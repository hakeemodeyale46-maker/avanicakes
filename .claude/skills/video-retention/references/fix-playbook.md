# Fix Playbook — TikTok · Instagram · Facebook

Every fix must be concrete enough that an editor could do it without asking a question, and
must name the platform it's for.

## What can be changed, and when

Short-form posts generally **can't be re-edited after posting** — the video file is fixed.
What's usually possible (varies by platform and changes over time; check the app):

| Change | After posting | Next post / repost |
|---|---|---|
| Caption text, hashtags, location | ✅ Usually editable (Instagram, Facebook; TikTok has been limited — check app) | ✅ |
| Cover image | ⚠ Sometimes (Instagram grid cover; TikTok limited after posting) | ✅ |
| Pinned comment / first comment (links, order info, context) | ✅ Yes | ✅ |
| Reply to comments (incl. TikTok video replies) | ✅ Yes — can create follow-up content | ✅ |
| Share to Stories / groups / DMs, boost (paid) | ✅ Yes | ✅ |
| Cut, re-time, change text overlays, captions, sound, pacing | ❌ Needs a **re-edit and new post** | ✅ |

So most fixes are labelled **REPOST/RE-EDIT** (a new, improved version — often worth doing for
a strong idea that underperformed, especially on a different platform or after some time) or
**NEXT VIDEO** (lessons for future posts). Avoid posting near-identical duplicates back to back
on the same account; change the hook, cut, and caption meaningfully.

## Fix card format (required)

```
[#] <name>                  Platform: TT / IG / FB / ALL   Priority: H/M/L   Confidence: H/M/L
Where:   <timestamp range | first frame | text overlay #N | caption | cover | sound>
Problem: <viewer-level description>
Pacing:  <speed + density verdict, if relevant>
Evidence:<data point and/or content observation>
Fix:     <exact action; for words, write the words>
Why:     <psychology reason for this platform's audience>
Expect:  <metric that should move + how to verify>
Scope:   THIS POST | REPOST/RE-EDIT | NEXT VIDEO
```

Priority = (expected impact × confidence) ÷ effort. Fixes to the **first failing funnel stage**
come first.

## Stop fixes (first frame — the scroll stopper)

- The first frame is the thumbnail. Start mid-action or on the most striking image.
- Readable at phone size: one focal point, high contrast, the subject (e.g., the cake) big in
  frame.
- Text hook in the safe zone — not under the caption area at the bottom or the buttons on the
  right.
- **TikTok**: movement + spoken hook from frame one.
- **Instagram**: beautiful + clear muted; choose a grid cover that fits the profile.
- **Facebook**: must tell the story muted — clear image + large, plain text line.

## Hook fixes (first ~1–3 seconds)

1. **Delete everything before the hook** — no "hey guys", no logo, no intro, no "so today…".
2. **Confirm the promise immediately** — show what the text/caption promises.
3. **Add stakes or a question** — why it matters, what could go wrong.
4. **Show the payoff first** (result-first) or tease it, then show how.
5. **Start in motion.**

Hook templates (adapt; always true):
- **Result first**: "This $15 cake fooled a wedding planner."
- **Stakes**: "The order is due in 2 hours and the top tier just cracked."
- **Contrast**: "Beginners frost like this. Bakers frost like this."
- **Question**: "Why do homemade cakes sink in the middle?"
- **Mistake**: "Stop putting your cake in the fridge like this."
- **Story opener**: "A customer called crying at 7am…"
- **POV** (TikTok/IG): "POV: you promised your daughter a unicorn cake."

**Adapt per platform** — always give each version:
- **TikTok**: short, punchy, casual, can be playful or trend-based. ("POV: the cake leaned 🫠")
- **Instagram**: short, clean, aspirational or relatable. ("The cake that took 14 hours.")
- **Facebook**: plain, warm, complete, context-rich, no slang. ("This bride's grandmother
  baked her wedding cake in 1972. Today we recreated it.")

## Pacing fixes

See `pacing.md` for the full table. Always say the current pace, the platform's target, and the
exact edit. Common ones:
- **Too slow** → cut pauses/filler, start shots later, timelapse process, move payoff earlier.
- **Too fast** → hold key shots longer, slow voiceover, fewer ideas, pause before the reveal.
- **Too little going on** → add a visual change, a clear text line, purposeful sound.
- **Too much going on** → remove layers (stickers, SFX, second text box, loud music under
  speech); make text and voice say the same thing.
- **Unreadable text** → fewer words, larger, higher contrast, on screen longer
  (~0.3–0.4s/word + 1s younger audiences; ~0.5s/word + 1.5s for older/Facebook).

## Body fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Steady slide | Cut 10–30% of runtime; remove repeated points; keep one open question running |
| Drops at section changes | End each section with a forward hook ("but that wasn't the worst part…"); remove "so that's step one" language |
| Long talking-to-camera stretch | Add B-roll of the process, punch-ins, text; per-platform frequency in `pacing.md` |
| Confusion / rewinds | Slow down, label on screen, show before telling |
| Repetition | Cut the second explanation |
| Payoff too early | Move the reveal later, or add a bigger second question |
| False ending ("so that's it") | Remove it; replace with a hook to the last part |
| Energy dip | Cut, compress into a montage, or re-record |
| Only works with sound (FB/IG) | Burn in captions; add text telling the story |

## Payoff & ending fixes

- Deliver the promise clearly and give the reveal room (hold 2–4s; 3–5s on Facebook).
- End quickly after the payoff.
- **TikTok/Instagram**: design a loop, or end on an honest open question.
- **Facebook**: give a complete, satisfying ending.
- CTA at the peak, one sentence, platform-appropriate:
  - TT: "Comment 'recipe' and I'll post it" / "Part 2 tomorrow" (only if true).
  - IG: "Send this to someone who needs a birthday cake" / "Save for later".
  - FB: "Share this with someone who loves baking" / "Message us to order for your family".

## Caption fixes (all platforms)

- First line carries the hook (it's what shows before "more").
- **TikTok**: short, searchable keywords (what it is + city for local).
- **Instagram**: hook line, value/story, CTA, a few relevant hashtags, location tag.
- **Facebook**: a short, warm story in plain language; how to order/price/location; tag or ask
  a personal question to spark comments.

## Satisfaction fixes

- **Share trigger**: relatable moment, surprising result, heartwarming story, "tag someone".
- **Save trigger**: recipes, steps, lists, price guides — say "save this".
- **Comment trigger**: a specific, easy question tied to the video.
- **Business trigger**: clear how-to-order info in the video, caption, and pinned comment.

## Anti-patterns (don't recommend)

- Bait the video doesn't deliver; fake "part 2"; fake giveaways.
- Engagement bait as the main tactic ("comment YES if…").
- Random cuts/SFX with no purpose — speed without substance.
- Posting the TikTok cut unchanged on Facebook (or vice versa).
- Leaving another platform's watermark on the video.
- One universal "ideal length" — use the shortest complete version for each platform.
- Generic advice not tied to a timestamp, frame, or metric.

## Next-video experiment design

- Change **one major variable** at a time (hook style, pace, length, first frame, CTA).
- Define the metric that should move (TikTok watched-full %, Instagram skip rate, Facebook
  3-second views ÷ reach, shares per 1,000 views).
- Compare over 3–5 videos per platform, not one.
- Keep a simple log: platform, video, variable changed, result.
